Pricing

Changes apply to new subscriptions; existing subscribers keep the price they signed up at. Every change needs a fresh authenticator code (if you just signed in, wait for the next code) and is written to the audit log. Times are West Africa Time.

Prices and standing discounts

The list price is what a plan costs before any discount. The standing discount is shown to customers as a discount line (for example 15% off annual). Campaigns sit on top of it.
