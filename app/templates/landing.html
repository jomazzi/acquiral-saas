Payroll

Payroll under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025

Run payroll without spreadsheet macros. Acquiral calculates PAYE under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, pension and NHF for each run, produces PDF payslips, and posts the run to your books. Payroll is included on the Organisation plan.

  • PAYE calculated under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025
  • Pension and NHF deductions for each employee
  • PDF payslips for every employee
  • Other African payroll frameworks are planned
