How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your subscription is active and for 30 days afterwards, then deleted, and it leaves our backups within a further 35 days. Billing and tax records are kept for the period the law requires. Security and error logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted.

Security

We use HTTPS, a firewall, database-level separation between customers' workspaces, salted password hashing, encryption of stored payment-provider keys, email verification of new accounts, rate limiting of sign-up and password-reset requests, role-based access, and regular backups. No system is completely secure; if a breach is likely to put your rights at risk we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within the time the law requires.

Cookies and fonts

One session cookie keeps you signed in; it is strictly necessary. We use no advertising or analytics cookies. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers.
