This addendum applies where {{ L.company }} processes personal data on your behalf through {{ app_name }}. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you are the data controller and {{ L.company }} is the data processor for the data in your workspace.

1. What we process

  • Your users: names, usernames, email addresses.
  • Your employees (payroll): names, pay data, tax IDs, pension and housing fund numbers, bank details.
  • Your customers, vendors, donors and funders: names, contact details, invoices, bills and payments.
  • Your financial records: journals, bank statements and reconciliation data, attachments.

Purpose: providing, supporting and securing the Service. Duration: while your subscription is active, plus the 30-day export period and the deletion periods in our Terms.

2. Our obligations

  • We process personal data only on your documented instructions (these terms and your use of the Service), unless the law requires otherwise.
  • Personnel with access are bound by confidentiality.
  • We apply the security measures in section 4.
  • We help you respond to data subject requests and meet your own obligations, at reasonable cost.
  • We notify you without undue delay, and within 48 hours of discovery, of a personal data breach affecting your data, so you can meet the 72-hour duty to notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
  • We delete or return your data at the end of the services, as set out in our Terms.
  • We provide information reasonably needed to show compliance and allow one audit or security questionnaire per year on reasonable notice.

4. Security measures

  • HTTPS encryption in transit; a firewall limiting open ports.
  • Database-level row security, so one customer's records cannot be read by another customer's session.
  • Passwords stored as salted hashes; customers' own payment-provider keys stored encrypted.
  • Email verification for new accounts; role-based access; rate limiting of sign-up and password-reset requests.
  • Daily database backups with limited retention.
  • Production access limited to authorised staff.
  • The operations console has database permissions limited to account and billing information, requires two-factor authentication, and logs every action.

5. International transfers

Data is hosted in {{ L.hosting_location }} and processed by the sub-processors above. We rely on contractual protections consistent with the Act's requirements for cross-border transfers. You confirm you have any consent or legal basis needed for the transfer of your data subjects' data.

6. Your responsibilities

You decide what personal data to enter, have a lawful basis for it, give employees, donors and others any notices required, and control who in your organisation has access.
